A home in the hills, run by a host who treats you like family

Kote Homestay sits among forest, fields and misty hills in the Western Ghats. The rooms and washrooms are kept clean and hygienic, and the sound of flowing water is never far away.

Your host is always around: giving directions to the falls, joining the evening campfire and keeping a first-aid kit at hand. Guests arrive as travellers and leave as friends.

Questions guests ask us

Not here? Message the owner on WhatsApp and you will get a personal reply.

Where is Kote Homestay?

Kote Homestay is in the Western Ghats of Karnataka, near Chikkamagaluru. Kodige Falls is 2 km away and Bandaje Falls is 10 km away. Open the map in the Find us section, or message us on WhatsApp for directions.

How far are Kodige Falls and Bandaje Falls?

Kodige Falls is about 2 km from the homestay and Bandaje Falls is about 10 km. Ask your host for the best time of day to go and how to get there safely.

What can we do at the homestay?

Play cricket, volleyball, badminton and lagori on the open grounds, and gather around the campfire in the evening. Both waterfalls are a short trip away.

Are the rooms and washrooms clean?

Yes. We keep every room and washroom clean and hygienic, and the photos on this page show them as they are.

Is Kote Homestay suitable for families and groups?

Yes. We have a family and group room with several beds, plus open grounds for games, so families and groups of friends can stay together.

Is first aid available?

Yes. The owner keeps a first-aid kit at the homestay and is on hand to help.

How do I book a stay?

Choose your dates in the booking bar or the enquiry form and send it on WhatsApp. The owner replies personally to confirm availability and rates. You do not need to pay to enquire.

What are the rates?

Rates depend on the season, the room and the size of your group. Send an enquiry and the owner will share the tariff.
